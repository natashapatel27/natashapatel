GROWL Task Force

Joining
LAB UC San Diego Gravitational Wave Paleontology Lab FOCUS population-synthesis catalogs

GROWL (GRavitatiOnal-Wave paLeontoLogy) is a push to build a standardized, public catalog of population-synthesis simulations for gravitational-wave sources — an attempt to break the field's "simulation silos" so different codes and assumptions can actually be compared. The task force pairs a Discord research community with a self-paced, roughly ten-module onboarding into gravitational-wave astrophysics.
